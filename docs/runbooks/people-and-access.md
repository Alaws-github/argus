# People and access

**Who:** admins. Where: account menu, **People and roles**.

## Add a person

1. **Add person**.
2. Enter their full name (must be unique), email, role and a temporary password of at least 10 characters.
3. Give them the password directly. They can change it under **Your account**.

## Choose a role

| Role | Give it to |
| --- | --- |
| Admin | Whoever manages the workspace, people and AI models |
| Engagement lead | Whoever runs an engagement and signs off the report |
| Reviewer | Senior team members who approve stages and review tests |
| Preparer | Team members who do the testing and gather evidence |
| Viewer | Anyone who only needs to read: client contacts, quality reviewers |

## Change a role, reset a password, remove access

- **Change a role**: pick the new role in the person's row. It takes effect on their next action.
- **Reset a password**: **Reset password**, enter a temporary one. All their sessions end.
- **Remove access**: **Disable**. People are never deleted, so their name stays on the approvals they made. **Enable** restores access.

You can't disable yourself or remove your own admin role.

## Someone changes their name

Anyone can change their display name under **Your account**. Their old names stay attached to them, so they still can't approve work they signed under an old name, and nobody else can take that name.
