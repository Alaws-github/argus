# Plan a use case with the AI

**Who:** anyone who prepares work (preparer, reviewer, lead, admin). **You need:** an engagement you can see.

1. Click **+ Add use case** in the top bar. The Plan page opens with **Plan with AI** selected.
2. Describe the AI in a sentence or two: what it does and who it affects. Press Enter to send.
3. Answer the Use Case Planner's questions. It asks for what is still missing: a name, the business owner, the data it uses, the people affected, how much it acts on its own, and the vendor.
4. Watch the **Brief** on the right fill in. Fields the Planner filled have an **AI** tag. Fix anything that isn't right by typing in the field; from then on that field is yours and the Planner leaves it alone.
5. Set the **Kind of AI** and **Priority** if the Planner didn't.
6. When **Still needed** disappears, click **Add to Intake**. The card is created under your name. Someone else decides whether it goes forward.

**Prefer to type it yourself?** Click **Fill it in myself** at the top. Same brief, no chat. Or use **+ Quick add** at the top of the Intake column on the board.

**Come back later.** Drafts stay on the left of the Plan page. **+ New plan** starts another; **Delete draft** removes one that hasn't been added.

**If the Planner says it's switched off**, a lead has turned it off for this engagement (Engagement settings > Agents > Use Case Planner). Fill the brief in yourself.

**Better drafting.** On the offline rules the Planner only files your answers and spots obvious things from keywords. A lead can give it a model in Engagement settings > Agents.
