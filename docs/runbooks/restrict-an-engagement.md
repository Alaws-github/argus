# Make an engagement members-only

**Who:** a lead or admin.

1. Switch to the engagement, then go to **More > Settings > General**.
2. Under **Who can see this engagement**, choose **Members only** and confirm. You are added as a member automatically.
3. Add the people who should see it with **Add a member**.

From then on, people who aren't members (other than admins) don't see the engagement in the switcher, and its use cases, evidence, issues, incidents, audit entries and live updates answer "not found" to them. To undo it, choose **Everyone signed in**. A members-only engagement must keep at least one member.
