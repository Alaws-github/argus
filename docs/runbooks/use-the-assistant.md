# Use the assistant

**Who:** anyone who prepares or reviews work (viewers can read but not ask). **You need:** nothing; it works offline. For full answers an admin picks its model on the Models page.

1. On a use case, click **Ask about this**. On a register (Risks, Controls, Issues) or anywhere else, click **Ask** in the top bar.
2. Pick a starter question or type your own, for example "What should I check before approving?" or "Which high risks have no control?".
3. Read the answer. Click a source chip (K-number) to read the passage it relied on.
4. If it suggests a change, read what it would do and why, then:
   - **Apply** to make the change under your own name. It is refused if your role couldn't make it by hand, and the reason is shown.
   - **Discard** to set it aside.
5. If the answer lists "things the harness removed", the assistant tried to cite a passage it wasn't given or to change a record it wasn't shown. Treat the rest of that answer with care.

The assistant never approves, signs off, concludes a test, accepts a risk or closes an issue. Every applied change appears in the audit log twice: the change itself, and a note that the assistant proposed it and who applied it.
