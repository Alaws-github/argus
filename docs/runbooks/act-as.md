# Run the whole flow as one person (act as)

**Who:** an admin. **Use for:** demos, testing and training. **Not for:** client engagements.

1. Go to **More > Workspace settings > Sign-in & security**.
2. Read the warning, then turn on **Allow admins to act as someone else**.
3. Open your account menu (your initials, top right). Under **Act as**, pick a person.
4. A blue banner shows **Acting as** that person, and who you really are. You now have their role: as a preparer you can add use cases but not approve them.
5. Switch to someone else from the account menu at any time, for example a reviewer to approve what you just added as the preparer. Dragging a card to the next column works too.
6. Click **Stop acting** in the banner to be yourself again.

**What gets recorded.** Every action is recorded under the person you acted as, and the audit log adds your real name beside it. Starting and stopping are recorded too. **Verify the chain** covers all of it.

**Why it's off by default.** Segregation of duties checks names. While you can switch names, one real person can both raise and approve work. Turn it off again before using the workspace on a client engagement.

**Changing someone's role** doesn't need act as: in **Workspace settings > People & roles**, use the role list on their row.
