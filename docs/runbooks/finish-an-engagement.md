# Finish an engagement

**Who:** the engagement lead, plus a second lead or admin to sign off.

1. Check the **Controls, Traceability** tab for gaps, and the **Issues** page for anything open or overdue.
2. Go to **Report**. For each of the seven areas, read the suggested score and its reasons, choose your score (1 to 5) and write why. **Save** each.
3. Read the report below the scores. Download it as **Markdown** or use **Print or save PDF**.
4. A lead who didn't score every area clicks **Sign off**. Changing any score afterwards clears the sign-off.
5. When the work is delivered, archive the engagement: [Archive or delete](archive-or-delete.md). Download the full record first if you need to keep it outside the app.
