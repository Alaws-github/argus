# Report and close an incident

**Who:** anyone who prepares work reports and updates it; a reviewer or lead who didn't report it closes it.

1. Go to **Issues > Incidents** and click **Report an incident**.
2. Say what happened, how serious it is, when it happened and **when you became aware**. Deadlines count from that moment.
3. Link the AI system and use case if you know them, and name an owner.
4. Tick who must be told. The harness works out each deadline:
   - Jamaica Data Protection Act personal data breach: the Information Commissioner within 72 hours, and the people affected.
   - EU AI Act serious incident: within 15 days; 2 days for a widespread infringement or critical infrastructure; 10 days where someone died.
   - Your own internal escalation: 24 hours.
   Confirm what applies with the client's legal team; these are a guide.
5. As you work, add notes to the **Timeline**, mark it **Contained** and then **Resolved**.
6. When each notice goes out, type its reference (email, case number) and click **Record as sent**. Sending late is recorded as late.
7. Raise **Corrective actions**. Each becomes an issue on the Issues page, with an owner and a due date.
8. Write the **root cause** and the **lessons**.
9. A reviewer or lead who didn't report it clicks **Close incident**. The harness refuses until every notice is sent and every corrective action is closed or accepted.
