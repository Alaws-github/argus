# First-time setup

**Who:** the first admin. **You need:** the app running ([Install and run](install-and-run.md)).

1. Open the app. It shows **Create the first admin**.
2. Enter your full name, email and a password of at least 10 characters. Your name is how your approvals appear in the audit log.
3. Add your colleagues: account menu (your initials), **People and roles**, **Add person**. See [People and access](people-and-access.md). Add at least one other reviewer or lead: segregation of duties means nobody can approve their own work.
4. Connect an AI model, or keep using the free built-in rules: see [Add a model provider](add-a-model-provider.md).
5. Set the monthly spending limit on the **Models** page (More, Models).
6. Try the demo: open the board, click **AI-2 Customer service chatbot**, and take it forward. Or start a real engagement: [Start a new engagement](new-engagement.md).
